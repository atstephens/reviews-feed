NOW SHOWING : INDEPENDENT THEATER REVIEWS

Mission: Keep cinema alive!

I’ve embarked on a journey to explore different independent theaters around San Francisco and review them! As a film major, I’ve always had such
a close relationship with cinemas and feel compelled to help keep the cinema alive. I’ve made it my mission to see at least two movies each month. However, I have gotten incredibly bored of seeing Nicole Kidman constantly before a movie and needed a change! Why not seek change AND
support local business?! So I knew that I wanted to start spending more time at independent theaters and get some information about them to help
people not only see the variety of independent theaters we have, but also the locations and style to show them AMC is not the only answer!
Now, let’s explore The Balboa Theater, Vogue Theatre, The Roxie Theater, and a little honorable mention…
